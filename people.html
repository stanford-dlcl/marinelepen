Decoding Marine Le Pen's Rhetoric is the parent website for the book Marine Le Pen prise aux mots. Décryptage du nouveau discours frontiste (Seuil, 2014).

People

This website is the continuation of a collective project that culminated in the publication of Marine Le Pen prise aux mots. It too intends to foster collaborative work in the future. 

In addition to the authors, a team of undergraduate students, graduate students, librarians, technology specialists, computer science enginers, editors, copy-editors and graphic designers all worked in small and big ways towards the publication of the book. Students who participated in the compilation of the database are: Lucile Bougon, Mary Ann Toman-Miller, Sruti Sarathy, Sayeh Fattahi, Devangi Vivrekar, Magali Duque, Fatoumata Seck, Michaela Hulstyn, Anja Young. Laurent Mérat and Mike Widner provided invaluable technical support. 

Professor Jean-Pierre Dupuy had a decisive role as perspicatious reader and editor.
